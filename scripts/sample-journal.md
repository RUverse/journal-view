=== 3 years ago ===
mood: lonely
tags: home

Eleven days in the flat. Everything is unpacked except the box marked MISC, which I'm avoiding.

I worked out tonight that the only people I've spoken to since the movers left are the man at the corner shop, a woman at the bus stop who wanted to know if the 14 had gone, and Mum, twice. The man at the corner shop now says "just the milk?" before I've put it on the counter. That is currently the closest thing I have to a friend in this city, and I was more pleased about it than I'd admit to anyone.

Sent three more applications. One I actually want: a small studio that designs signage and wayfinding for hospitals. The other two are banks. The freelance work covers the rent for now, but I spend all day not talking to anyone and then reply to a client's email with alarming warmth.

What I want, written down so I can check later: two or three people here I could ring on a bad day. Work where I can point at something and say that's better because I did it. Not much to ask. Apparently a lot to arrange.

Walked down to the river after dinner. The city is nicer than I'm giving it credit for. I just don't know where anything is yet.
